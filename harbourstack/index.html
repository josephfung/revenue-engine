Revenue Engine Maturity — Case Study

HarbourStack

A workshop exercise for diagnosing revenue engine maturity across five dimensions.

Background

Scenario

HarbourStack is a 6-year-old SaaS company that helps mid-sized logistics and field service companies coordinate scheduling, dispatch, compliance paperwork, and customer updates.

They grew to $3.8M ARR mostly through founder-led sales, referrals, and a handful of strong early customer wins. The founder could walk into almost any sales conversation and connect the product to the buyer's pain within 10 minutes.

Over the last 18 months, HarbourStack hired a VP Sales, four AEs, two SDRs, and a marketing lead. They also implemented HubSpot, added call recording, built dashboards, and started running paid campaigns.

At first, things looked promising:

  • Pipeline increased from $1.4M to $4.2M
  • Demo volume doubled
  • CRM reporting became much cleaner
  • The team finally had defined sales stages
  • The VP Sales introduced weekly pipeline reviews

But growth did not accelerate the way the board expected.

Symptoms

What the team is seeing

  • New reps can book meetings, but struggle to move deals past discovery
  • The founder still gets pulled into late-stage deals to "tell the story properly"
  • Sales cycles have stretched from 54 days to 91 days
  • Discounting has increased
  • Marketing says campaign volume is up, but conversion quality is uneven
  • Sales says many leads are "technically qualified" but not actually good-fit buyers
  • Customer Success says new customers often arrive with unclear expectations
  • Expansion revenue is weak, even though several customers say they like the product
  • Churn has crept up from 9% to 17% gross revenue churn
  • The happiest customers seem to share a pattern, but the team has not clearly named it
Leadership Debate

What leadership wants to do

The board recommends professionalizing sales.

The VP Sales wants two more AEs and a better outbound tool.

Marketing wants more paid budget — top-of-funnel metrics look healthy and "we just need more shots on goal."

The founder is unsure. They can feel something is off, but the dashboards make it look like the team is doing the right things.

Exercise

Your task

Where is HarbourStack least mature? Pick the weakest one or two dimensions:

  1. Market Fit
  2. Sales Process
  3. Enablement
  4. Technology
  5. Retention & Expansion
Discussion prompt

What should HarbourStack fix before adding more sales capacity?
